# KRYLO Community Conduct

KRYLO welcomes contributors who communicate respectfully, review evidence carefully, and prioritize user safety.

Expected behavior:

- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Give specific, actionable feedback.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and security concerns.
- Respect privacy and confidential information.
- Credit prior work and follow licenses.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or deliberate disruption.

Maintainers may remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when behavior harms contributors or project safety. Security reports should use private disclosure channels rather than public issue threads.
