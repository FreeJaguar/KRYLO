# Third-Party Notices

KRYLO Core is designed to run without bundling third-party orchestration frameworks, MCP servers, language servers, or development tools.

Optional integrations remain separately installed products owned by their respective publishers. KRYLO may detect and invoke them only under the active trust, version, data-egress, and environment policies.

Before a release, generate a dependency and license inventory for every package actually bundled in KRYLO. Include notices required by transitive dependencies and copied assets.

Do not copy third-party prompts, skills, scripts, rules, or documentation into KRYLO without:

- Confirming the license.
- Recording the source and commit.
- Preserving required notices.
- Reviewing security implications.
- Explaining why an adapter is insufficient.

The names of third-party products referenced in documentation remain the property of their respective owners.
